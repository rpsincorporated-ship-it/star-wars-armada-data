# Rapid Reinforcements II Audit Correction — Milestone 3.3a

**Status:** PASS

General Draven was incorrectly classified as an Officer in Milestone 3.3.
The official Rapid Reinforcements v2.3 card is a Rebel Commander.

Corrected RRII distribution:
- 2 Commanders: General Draven, Anakin Skywalker
- 2 Officers: Governor Pryce, Asajj Ventress
- 4 Squadrons: Fenn Rau, Vult Skerris, Matchstick, Wat Tambor

No production card data was modified.
