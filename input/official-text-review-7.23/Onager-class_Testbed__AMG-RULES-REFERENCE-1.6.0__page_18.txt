the player edges. Place the setup area markers at the corners of the
play area when playing with these objectives. The setup area is the
entire play area. Each player’s deployment zone is within the distance
measured by the length of two range rulers from that player’s edge (see              Short Player Edges Diagram
diagram on page 18).
